I’m in my fourth-year of studying MEng Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence at the University of Southampton whilst also working on flexible hours as an AI Software Engineer at Spectrum IT Recruitment in Chilworth.

I have a particular interest in AI and Machine Learning, having specialised in the field during the latter half of my degree. For my third-year independent project, I investigated the explainability of deep learning biometric age estimation systems and evaluated them for inherent biases - work I am currently writing up for publication in my spare time. Currently, in my fourth-year group project, I am collaborating with an industry partner to evaluate the state of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems and determine their ongoing value in the modern landscape of agentic AI.

Alongside research, I am passionate about software development. Having built multiple projects across both academic and professional settings, I thrive on collaborating directly with end-users and asking the right questions to turn their vision into reliable, working software which meets their desired outcomes. For me, it’s important to take the time to design clean, logical architectures and put real thought into how the codebase and underlying data models are structured so that the software is robust, scalable, and easy to maintain.

Outside of work I enjoy playing piano (mostly jazz and prog rock), going to the gym alongside running to keep fit, and playing snooker with my friends as well as in the university snooker league.

StockWeb

A knowledge graph of inter-company relationships combined with stress-tested algorithmic trading
